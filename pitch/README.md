# Pitch

The HackYeah 2026 pitch, one deck per track. Each deck has 10 slides and tells the same story: three people looking for a home in Kraków, the problem, and how the app answers it.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `Smart_City_Presentation.html`, `.pdf` | The Smart City track: the app, its data and the city as host |
| `Artificial_Intelligence_Presentation.html`, `.pdf` | The Artificial Intelligence track: the same story, with the guarded AI summary at the centre |
| `Smart_City_Cover_Image.png`, `Artificial_Intelligence_Cover_Image.png` | Cover images for the submission, 1920 x 1080 |

Open an HTML deck in a browser. The arrow keys or a click move through the slides, `N` shows the narration and `F` goes full screen. Items build in one by one. The PDF is the same deck with every item shown and no narration.

- **Real data only.** Every screen is a screenshot of the app, and every map and number is the app's output for Kraków on 4 October 2026. The AI answer on the slides is a real answer of the city service, with its AI label.
- **Credits.** The street blueprint and the district shapes are drawn from OpenStreetMap data, © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL 1.0). The credit is on each slide and cover that uses them.
- **AI use.** The decks were written and laid out with the help of Claude Code, and reviewed by the team, as `../README.md` discloses.
