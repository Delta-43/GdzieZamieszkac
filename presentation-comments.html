Choosing a flat is easy to research. Choosing the district around it is not. Transport, air, noise, green space, road accidents and prices are published by different institutions, in different formats, with different dates. Listing sites show offers, not what a district is like. (Click) We bring all of it into one page per district. Three kinds of data, always labelled.

  • ObservedMeasured or officially reportedsale prices from the national register
  • EstimatedCalculated from real inputsair quality between monitoring stations
  • ProxyAn indirect indicatornight noise from bars and clubs
We never invent a number, but not every number is a direct measurement. (Click) Observed values are measured or officially reported, such as sale prices from the national register. (Click) Estimated values are calculated from real inputs, such as air quality interpolated between monitoring stations. (Click) Proxies are indirect indicators, such as night noise inferred from bars and clubs per square kilometre. The label always travels with the value.

Each square is one metric in our catalogue. Kraków has data for 45 of 51. (Click) The six gaps are not zeros. Each one shows its reason. The biggest: Kraków has no recorded crime data by district, so safety uses road accidents and proxies. We report road accident indicators and make no safety verdict. The data was audited on 30 September with zero errors and zero warnings.

Code computes the score. AI only tells the story.

AI-written report · stored
“Stare Miasto scores 40.9 out of 100, placing it 16th among Kraków's 18 districts.”

A report with any number not in the facts is rejected.

(Click) The score is computed in code: percentile ranks within Kraków, from 0 to 100, each category counting equally, or with your own weights. It is tested. (Click) A language model only narrates the facts, in Polish and in English, 36 reports in total. They are written ahead of time, stored, and labelled as AI-written in the app. (Click) A guard rejects any report that contains a number that is not in the facts.

We show price history, not forecasts.

  • We tested two forecasting methods
  • Neither beat the naive baseline
  • So we publish history and the backtest
People want to know where prices are going. (Click) We tested two forecasting methods. (Click) Neither beat the naive baseline. (Click) So the portal shows quarterly sale price history since 2021, momentum, the city's past range and the backtest, and never a predicted number.

What does the city get? (Click) A free portal it can run on its own servers, built on its own open data and national registers. The backend ships as a Docker image. (Click) It is read-only and stores nothing about its users: no accounts, cookies, analytics or third-party requests. (Click) It is built to WCAG 2.2 level AA, as Polish law requires of public bodies, with a draft accessibility statement for the city to complete. (Click) And every gap says why, which tells the city which datasets to publish next, such as crime figures by district.
