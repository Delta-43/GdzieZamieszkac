GdzieZamieszkać means "where to live". We help people choose a district of Kraków before they start looking for a flat, and we built it as a public service the city can host.

The idea in one sentence

GdzieZamieszkać helps you choose a district before you look at flats.

It is not
  • a listings site
  • brokers or agents
  • links to offers
Who it is for

Residents and newcomers: singles, couples, families, seniors, students.

Honest by design

  • District level, not address. An address score implies precision the data lacks.
  • No price forecast. Two methods failed against naive baselines. We publish history and the backtest.
  • “Recorded crimes per 10 000 residents.” Never “safe” or “dangerous”.
  • Limits shown openly, in the app.
This is probably our strongest differentiator. Most tools show numbers with no explanation. (Click) Every value carries its kind (observed, estimated or proxy), its source, licence, date, method and caveat. Where there is no data, the app says why and shows no zero. (Click) And we admit limits. We score districts, not addresses, because an address score would imply precision the data does not have. We show no price forecast: we tested two methods and neither beat a naive baseline, so we publish the history and the backtest. Safety wording is recorded crimes per 10 000 residents; the app never says safe or dangerous. Admitting limits is why you can trust the rest. Be ready for the question on crime data: Kraków has none by district, so the safety category leans on road accidents and proxies, and says so.

AI writes the words. Code owns the numbers. Your data stays yours.

The role of AI

  1. Stored facts, computed in code
  2. A language model writes the district report
  3. A guard rejects any number not in the facts
  4. Every report carries an AI label

The personalised report is built in the city service.

Privacy and ethics

  • The backend stores nothing about users.
  • The household profile stays in the browser. planned
  • It sets weights only. It never filters by who lives in a district: a legal risk.
The AI task sheet wants a clear and checkable role for AI. (Click) Ours: a language model writes the district report from stored facts and may only cite the numbers it is given. A guard checks the text and rejects any number that is not in the facts. Every report carries an AI label. The personalised report, where you type what you need, is built in the separate city service, under the same guard. The score itself is never AI. (Click) Privacy: the backend is read-only and stores nothing about users. The household profile, once built, stays in the browser and only sets weights; it never filters by who lives in a district, because steering by demographics is a legal risk. What a city could add. And why we are different.

For the city concepts

  • Verified resident feedback: identity checked, salted token, so a landlord cannot trace a report
  • Anonymous demand counts by district

Built, not published: the feedback form and the personalised AI report, in the city service. Feedback stays unverified and never changes a score.
