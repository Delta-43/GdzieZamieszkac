AI never computes the score. Code does, and it is tested.

(Click) Each district gets a livability score from 45 metrics in seven categories: transport, demographics, livability, amenities, environment, cost and safety indicators. You set what matters to you, or pick a persona preset. (Click) The score is computed in code, from percentile ranks within Kraków, and it is tested. (Click) You get a ranking that names the metrics behind each place, so it is explainable. (Click) AI never computes the score. For the AI category, this is the trust point: the model has no say in the number.

Where there is no data

The app says why. It never shows a zero.

  • District level, not address levelan address score implies precision the data lacks
  • No price forecasttwo methods failed against naive baselines; we publish history and the backtest
  • Safety wording“recorded crimes per 10 000 residents”, never “safe” or “dangerous”

Known limits are shown in the app: rents are one snapshot of asking rents, and long commute times run fast.

This is probably our strongest differentiator, because most tools show numbers with no explanation. (Click) Every value shows its kind: observed, estimated or proxy. It also shows its source, licence, date, method and caveat. (Click) Where there is no data, the app says why and shows no zero. (Click) We score districts, not addresses, because an address score implies a precision the data does not have. (Click) We show no price forecast, because two methods failed against naive baselines; we publish the history and the backtest. (Click) On safety we say "recorded crimes per 10 000 residents" and never "safe" or "dangerous". Known limits are shown openly, and admitting them is why you can trust the rest. Check this wording against docs/DATA_SOURCES.md before the pitch: the data notes say Kraków has no recorded crime data by district yet, so safety uses road accidents and proxies.

(Click) A language model writes the district report from stored facts, in Polish and English, and every report carries an AI label. (Click) A guard checks the report: the model may only cite the numbers it was given, and a report with any other number is rejected. (Click) The personalised report, where you type your requirements, is built in the separate city service. It narrates computed facts and makes no safety verdict. Models run through OpenRouter, and we disclose them in the README. (Click) On privacy: the backend is read-only and stores nothing about its users. (Click) The household profile stays in the browser and only sets weights. (Click) It never filters by who lives in a district, because steering by demographics is a legal risk. Agree the AI wording as a team before the pitch: TODO.md lists it as an open decision. Suggested line: AI narrates and translates, code scores.
